About CivicLink

CivicLink is a community information and coordination tool built to make it easier for neighbors, volunteers, and local organizations to find and support each other. The platform is designed to:

  • Encourage community cooperation
  • Make useful resources easier to discover
  • Help people communicate their needs
  • Encourage volunteering and donations
  • Provide a centralized community information board
Disclaimer: CivicLink is a community information and coordination tool. Information shown on this platform may be user-submitted and should be verified before relying on it. For immediate emergencies, contact your local emergency services.
